# Claim Boundary

## Allowed public description

APFKA is a reduced active-particle feedback surrogate diagnostic archive. It may be used to discuss kinetic-arrest-like behavior, noise-assisted annealing windows, feedback-geometry dependence, frustration-boundary classification, rescue-stability behavior, and persistent hard-boundary behavior **inside tested toy-model / reduced-surrogate settings**.

## Core interpretation

The repository studies a limited theoretical question: in a simplified active-particle surrogate with internal attributes and mathematical information-feedback dynamics, when do modeled configurations appear arrested, noise-assisted, feedback-sensitive, rescue-stable, or persistently unrecovered?

This is a model-internal diagnostic question. It is not an experimental active-matter conclusion and not an engineering-control result.

## Forbidden claims

This repository does **not** claim:

- experimental validation in real active matter,
- validation of physical colloids, organisms, robots, crowds, swarms, traffic, societies, economies, or civilization-scale systems,
- material-design certification or industrial process readiness,
- public-policy applicability,
- crowd-control, swarm-control, behavioral-control, or civilization-engineering utility,
- safety-certified control design,
- a universal kinetic-arrest theorem,
- a formal proof of a phase transition,
- proof that local feedback is uniquely necessary for noise-induced annealing,
- or a complete hydrodynamic theory.

## Feedback-language caution

The term **information feedback control** is used only for a mathematical feedback term in a reduced active-particle surrogate. It must not be interpreted as a practical controller for people, crowds, robots, biological populations, public infrastructure, materials, or devices.

## Standard short wording

> This work is a claim-bounded reduced active-particle feedback surrogate. It reports model-internal diagnostics of kinetic-arrest-like behavior, noise-assisted annealing, feedback-geometry dependence, and frustration-boundary behavior in tested toy-model settings. It does not validate real active matter, biological/social systems, swarm/crowd-control technology, or a universal kinetic-arrest mechanism.

## Publication posture
