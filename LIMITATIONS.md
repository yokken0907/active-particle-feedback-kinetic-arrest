# Limitations

## Model limitations

- The model is a reduced active-particle / ABP-style toy surrogate.
- The internal attribute variable is abstract and does not represent a calibrated physical material, organism, robot, social actor, or crowd participant.
- The information-feedback term is a mathematical update rule inside the model, not an experimentally implemented controller.
- Interaction potentials, parameters, noise amplitudes, feedback geometry, and frustration settings are model choices rather than fitted experimental parameters.
- Finite-size and holdout ranges are limited to the tested configurations.

## Validation limitations

- No experimental active-matter data are fitted.
- No material, device, biological, social, crowd, swarm, or civilization system is validated.
- No hydrodynamic continuum limit is derived.
- No formal universality class is established.
- No rigorous theorem of kinetic arrest is proven.
- No safety, engineering, deployment, policy, or public-infrastructure recommendation is made.

## Reproducibility limitations

- The repository includes a reconstructed public ABP diagnostic reproducer.
- The reproducer is internally consistent with the public diagnostic figures but is not a bitwise recovery of all original exploratory code.
- The archive should be treated as a paper companion and public diagnostic reproduction path, not as a complete production simulation environment.

## Public interpretation limitation

The result is best read as a hypothesis-generating diagnostic archive about active-particle toy-model dynamics under feedback and noise. It should not be read as a practical control framework or real-world intervention method.
