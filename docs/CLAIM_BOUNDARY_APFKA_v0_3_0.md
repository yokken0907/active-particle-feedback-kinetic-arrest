# APFKA v0.3.0 Claim Boundary

## Status

Decision: `PASS-APFKA-V030-SYNTHESIS-LOCKED`  
Phase coverage: `7/7`

## Allowed claims

- Noise-window / annealing-score diagnostics within the implemented reduced active-particle surrogate.
- Feedback-geometry dependence within tested toy-model settings.
- Frustration-boundary and rescue-stability classification.
- Persistent hard boundary retained as negative evidence.

## Forbidden claims

- Experimental active-matter validation.
- Biological or social-system validation.
- Crowd-control, swarm-control, civilization-engineering, or public-policy interpretation.
- Universal kinetic-arrest or annealing mechanism.
- Local feedback as a uniquely necessary annealing generator.

## Locked synthesis

In the implemented reduced active-particle surrogate, a noise-assisted annealing window was observed across multiple tested conditions, and local feedback was sometimes competitive or beneficial relative to null controls. However, the effect was not uniquely attributable to local feedback, and high-frustration geometries produced first-class failure boundaries. By v0.2.6, formerly persistent boundaries split into rescue-stable and persistent-hard-boundary regimes under targeted relief, with extreme frustration remaining unrecovered in the tested setting.

## Public wording

This addendum reports a claim-bounded reduced active-particle surrogate audit. It documents noise-assisted annealing windows, feedback-geometry dependence, frustration-boundary rescue, and one persistent hard boundary in the tested settings. It does not validate active matter experiments or social/civilization systems.
