# APFKA v0.3.1 Claim Boundary

This integrated revision combines the earlier ABP-style model manuscript with the v0.2.x audit synthesis. It may claim only model-internal diagnostic findings inside the implemented reduced surrogate:

- noise-assisted annealing-window motifs,
- feedback-geometry dependence,
- frustration-boundary classification,
- rescue-stability and persistent-hard-boundary behavior,
- and reconstructed public diagnostics of the baseline model.

It must not be read as experimental active-matter validation, biological or social-system validation, crowd-control or swarm-control technology, civilization-engineering guidance, policy guidance, a universal kinetic-arrest theorem, or proof that local feedback is necessary for noise-induced annealing.
