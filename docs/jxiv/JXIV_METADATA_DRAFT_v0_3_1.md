## Abstract EN
This integrated revision combines an earlier active-particle feedback surrogate manuscript with a subsequent claim-boundary audit sequence. The manuscript preserves the model equations and reconstructed ABP diagnostics while adding frozen-window, feedback-geometry, frustration-boundary, rescue-stability, and persistent-hard-boundary audits. The resulting claim is limited to toy-model diagnostics of noise-assisted annealing windows and frustration-boundary behavior. It does not claim experimental active-matter validation, social-system validation, crowd-control or civilization-engineering use, or a universal kinetic-arrest theorem.
